"School began with a man under a tree, a man who did not know he was a teacher, discussing his realizations with a few others who did not know they were students. The students reflected on what was exchanged and how good it was to be in the presence of this man. They wished their children also to listen to such a man. Soon, space was needed, and the first school was built.

The establishment of school was inevitable because it was part of the desire to raise the standards of learning. But the demands of the institution, in time, became paramount, and the original spirit of the school was lost. The architectural space must be dedicated to recreating that original spirit, to foster the feeling of an environment where one can learn through inspiration, not merely through instruction."
